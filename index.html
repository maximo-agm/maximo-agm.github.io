FREE BROWSER EXTENSION

MARK THE WORDSYOU'RE
WATCHING FOR

Colour-coded word lists, combination rules, pattern rules, tooltips, folders, temporary highlights in any colour, a pause that ends by itself, and two reports that tell you what has gone quiet and what is making too much noise. No account, no server of mine, and the extension never tracks you. Runs on Firefox for Android as well as on the desktop.
