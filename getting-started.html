Highlight · Getting Started

Get set up in under a minute.

Highlight is a browser extension that reads the pages you visit and marks the words and phrases you have told it to watch for. There is no account to create and nothing to configure before it starts working.

01 — Install it

Add it to the browser you already use.

Chrome Web StoreAdd to Chrome v2.1.0 Microsoft Edge Add-onsAdd to Edge v2.3.1 Firefox Add-onsAdd to Firefox v2.3.1
02 — What it does

Three lines, and you have the whole idea.

01
It marks what matters

Create a list of words or phrases, pick a colour, and every match on every page you visit is highlighted — including content that loads as you scroll.

02
It can say something

Attach a tooltip, a notification or a link to a whole list, or to one specific word inside it. Rules can also fire only when several words appear together.

03
It stays with you

Nothing is sent to a server and there is no account. Your setup lives on your device, and on Edge and Firefox it can follow your own browser account between computers.

03 — Setup

Four steps, none of them a wizard.

01
Install it from your browser's store

Use the tile that matches your browser above. No sign-in, no account creation, and no setup wizard.

02
Pin the icon to your toolbar

If you don't see it after installing, open the puzzle-piece or extensions menu and pin Highlight so it stays visible.

03
Create your first list

Click the + button, name it, pick a colour, and add one word or phrase per line. You can paste a whole column straight in.

04
Browse normally

Every page you open is marked automatically. Hover a highlighted word to see its tooltip, if you gave it one; click to pin it open.

04 — What it looks like

The real interface, not a mock-up.

The popup — your lists, at a glance. Click the pencil to edit, the pause icon to disable a list without deleting it.

A highlighted match on a real page, with its tooltip pinned open.

05 — Worth knowing early

Five things that save you time later.

Right-click to add. Select any text on a page, right-click, and add it to an existing list or a brand-new one without opening the popup.

Accents are ignored by default. Searching for “e” also finds “é”, across Spanish, French, German, Nordic, Slavic, Lithuanian and Russian Cyrillic.

Scope a list to certain sites. Each list can be limited to particular domains, or blocked from them, and there is a global never-highlight list too.

Pause instead of deleting. Any list or rule can be paused, and there are shortcuts to pause everything or just the current tab.

Three languages. English, Spanish and Lithuanian, switchable at any time in Settings.
